THE RULES ARE SIMPLE
01

One player privately chooses a whole number from 1 to 100.

02

Beat the boundary

MATCH WINNING RULE

Lowest total guesses wins!

Both players get equal guessing turns. The game adds all guesses used by each player across their guessing rounds. The player with the lowest total wins. Equal totals mean a draw.

FIVE-LEVEL CLUE SYSTEM

Difference of more than 10:

TOO LOW / TOO HIGH

Difference of 1 to 10:

LOW / HIGH

Exact match:

CORRECT
